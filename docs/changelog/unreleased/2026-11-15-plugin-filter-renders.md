## Performance

- **Plug-in filter stacks no longer slow down with every filter added:** a stack with a filter that reads neighbouring pixels (a degrain, a sharpen) runs at the photo's full resolution once per edit, shared by the loupe, the preview, the grid's cell and an export (before, each ran it, all at once, and decodes of different sizes each ran their own), and a filter added at the end, or a change to the last filter, runs only that filter on what the filters before it made. A render that is replaced (a slider drag starting, the loupe moving to another photo) stops its plug-in filters where they are. On a 10 MP scan with a negative conversion and a degrain, adding the third and fourth filters went from 119 s and 154 s to 63 s and 66 s, and an edit after adding one from 12 s to under 1 s.
