## Film
- A film scanner's RGBI TIFF (four 16-bit samples, no ExtraSamples, written by VueScan or SilverFast) decodes as opaque RGB with the infrared pass as `Decoded::infrared` (and `SourceInfo::infrared`, through the geometry with `render_plane`), no longer as RGBA.
- Infrared dust removal from that pass (`FilmDust` in the develop settings: controls `filmDust.remove`, `filmDust.sensitivity`, `filmDust.expand`, Section Sensor): a Source-stage step before the plug-in filters fills the marked pixels in from their surroundings and puts the film's grain back (`lcc_pipeline::film_dust::inpaint`). The map (`infrared_map`) measures each pixel's dip in grain widths against the pass's own level with the dips left out, joins a speck's soft edge to its core, finds faint lines by averaging along strokes in twelve directions, and grows round; the picture's leak into the pass is taken out first, the red channel's dips measured the same way subtracted in the proportion fitted on detail brighter than its surroundings (`reference`, `red_of`); it is cached per pass and settings and keyed into the stage caches. `Overlay::Infrared` (the pass in grey, the marks red) and `Overlay::FilmDust` (the marks, white on black); renders with the step or these views take the CPU pipeline (`film_dust::needs_cpu`).
- `spot.filmDustMask {sensitivity, expand}` (Remove ▸ Edit as Mask) stores the marks as an Object mask (a `SegMask` over the uncropped frame, named "Film dust (infrared)", replaced on a re-run, selected) for the brush and `spot.fromMask`. `spot.findFilmDust` (heal spots on the marks; without the pass, a search of the picture) is a command only: the Remove panel shows no film-dust button for a scan without the pass.
