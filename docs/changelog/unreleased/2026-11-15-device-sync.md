## Highlights

- **Device sync rebuilt:** pair devices on the same network, then sync the whole catalog (one device's catalog is kept) or collections only. Each collection's owner allows None, Preview, Serve or Sync; other devices take None, Previews, Full Quality or Full Sync. Photos, originals or previews and their edits travel; pills on grid cells and collection rows show where each photo lives and which way changes still travel. Same app version on both devices; LAN only.
