## Library

- **Image Quality Scanner:** Library ▸ Image Quality Scanner… (`photo.qualityScan`; `<cli> quality-scan`, MCP through the same command) scores photos for technical quality — sharpness (`lcc_pipeline::quality`'s Laplacian-style block energy, the same measure Assisted Culling uses), noise (the grain in the photo's flattest areas against a clean-photo reference), exposure (the share of the frame that isn't clipped, plus how close its tones sit to a well-exposed middle) and JPEG compression artifacts (the 8-pixel grid's step against the photo's other edges; raw sources are not measured for it) — each 0–100 and combined into a 0–100 score by per-factor weights (0–10, default 1 each). The score, the four factor scores and the verdict are stored on the photo (catalog format 20), so sorting, filtering, smart collections and rules see them without scanning again: `SortKey::Quality`, `Filter::quality` (`accepted`, `pending`, `rejected`, `unscanned`), `qualityMin` / `qualityMax`, the Library Sort menu's Quality, and the filter bar's quality picker and score floor. The scan is a background batch in the activity stack, cancellable, measuring thumbnail-level sources a batch at a time on every core; a whole scan's scores are one undo step (`photo.qualityScanPump` writes it the frame it ends). Scope: the selection (or the photos in view), a folder (and its subfolders) or the whole catalog; **Unscanned only** by default, `rescan` measures everything again. **Pick Flags**: `accepted` (default 70) and `rejected` (default 25) map the score to Pick / Reject — inclusive, never crossing, the rejected cutoff winning a tie — written onto photos the user has not flagged when `applyFlags` is on (a photo whose flag the user set is never touched; a flag a scan wrote is the scan's own — `QualityScan::flag_written` — so a rescan may change it). Clips are skipped: a poster frame is not the clip's quality. Dialog: Library ▸ Image Quality Scanner… (scope, the two cutoffs, the flag switch, rescan, the four weights; remembered between openings).
