## Develop

- **Pixel Shift merge:** Photo ▸ Photo Merge ▸ Pixel Shift (`merge.pixelShift`, `<cli> merge pixel-shift`) merges the frames of a Nikon Z 8 pixel-shift series (16 shots) on the raw mosaics, before demosaicing, into a 16-bit LinearRaw DNG written next to frame 01, imported and stacked on its frames. The series is recognised from the maker notes (shot count, frame number, exposure counter, even spacing); a selection that is not one complete series, or whose result already exists, is refused with the reason before anything is decoded. The merged file carries frame 01's capture time, keywords and descriptive metadata, its lens data, its JPEG as a preview and a provenance block (the frames it was made from, kept in the catalog too, catalog format 19). It opens as the camera's raw does: white balance, camera profile, fitted colour look and tone curve are those of the NEFX of the same series (mean ΔE00 below 0.12 against NX Studio's merge with chromatic-aberration correction off). `Delete Merge Sources` (`merge.deleteSources`) moves the frames other than frame 01 to Recently Deleted. Docs: `docs/pixel-shift.md`.
