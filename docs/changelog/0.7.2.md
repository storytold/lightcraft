# 0.7.2

Changes since 0.7.0 (0.7.1 was not released). After each item, the matching upstream pull request:
`(↑ #n)` means a pull request was opened upstream for this change, `(⇠ #n)` means the change was ported
from an earlier upstream pull request, and `(no PR)` means there is none. Each upstream PR is marked
merged, open or closed.

## Highlights

- **Many more raw formats decode from sensor data:** compressed Olympus / OM ORF, compressed Samsung SRW,
  vertical- and grid-tiled CR3 C-RAW, Minolta MRW, Sony ARW version 1, packed Nikon NEF (Z 6II), pre-X FinePix
  SuperCCD RAF, Canon CRW, Epson ERF and Mamiya MEF. Verified camera models: **314**. (MRW ↑ #1040 open,
  ERF ⇠ #1087 open, MEF ⇠ #1105 open, others no PR)
- **AI masks:** Subject and Background masks use BiRefNet (pure Rust, downloaded only when you ask for it), and
  SAM 3 Object masks can load from a local folder, tested with the real weights. (no PR)
- **Auto-updater:** the app checks the release feed, downloads the new version and installs it. Contributed by
  @deunderscorepaul. (no PR)
- **Feedback from Lightroom Classic users:** Auto now adapts to each photo, Edit In explains why it failed,
  and there are Sync Settings and Sync Metadata plus Auto Sync. (↑ #1414 open, ↑ #1417 open, details below)
- **Slideshow video export** with music, and slideshow sound on Linux and BSD. (no PR)

## Develop and masking

- Auto adapts to each photo. Highlights, Shadows, Contrast, Whites and Blacks follow the photo's own tones. (↑ #1414 open)
- The Auto button is greyed out while Auto's values are applied. (no PR)
- The white balance row is now at the top of Light, above a Tone heading. (no PR)
- Synchronize Settings dialog with Check All / Check None. Develop has a sync bar with Previous, Sync… and an
  Auto Sync switch. (no PR)
- Groups in the preset and profile lists start folded, except the one that holds the current preset or profile. (no PR)
- Dehaze process V3: a patch dark channel and an eased response, with no black halos. (↑ #1283 open)
- Sky / Subject / Background / Landscape masks are computed from one analysis of the whole photo, so they are more
  accurate on the labelled test scenes. (↑ #1322 open)
- Per-mask Feather slider; brush A / B presets and a Density slider. (no PR)
- Arrow keys move the crop frame (⌥ resizes it) and the selected Remove spot. (no PR)
- Going to an earlier History step no longer adds a step. (⇠ #1246 merged)
- Lightroom HDR settings `crs:HDRMaxValue` / `crs:SDRBlend` are read, and the SDR rendition has an SDR Blend slider. (no PR)

## Import, export and formats

- New raw decoders: see Highlights. 31 popular bodies were verified, plus the Z 8 / Z 9 lossless NEF and 15 FinePix bodies. (no PR)
- AVIF import (pure Rust). (no PR)
- EXIF and XMP are read from HEIC, HEIF and AVIF files. (⇠ upstream #1208)
- Render to DNG: a new export format, plus Photo ▸ Render to DNG, which adds the rendered DNG stacked with its
  original. (⇠ #1047 merged, ↑ #1067 open)
- Lossy DNG export. (↑ #1010 open)
- Lightroom catalog import brings in History, snapshots (as Versions), more develop settings, and collection
  and date smart-collection rules. It reads tables row by row instead of capping their size. (↑ #1247 open, ↑ #1014 open)
- Slideshow export to video (H.264 + AAC or ProRes + PCM, with your music). (no PR)
- Edit in PhotoCraft tells you why it didn't open, and finds PhotoCraft on macOS (Applications folders, Spotlight)
  and on Linux (PATH, AppImages). (↑ #1417 open)

## Library

- "Album" is now "Collection" throughout the UI. (no PR)
- Sync Metadata, and Sync Metadata / Sync Settings buttons in Library. (no PR)
- Up / Down move through the grid's rows. (⇠ #1264 merged)
- Delete asks before deleting by default, from the context menu too. (upstream #1266 closed; fork only)
- Plug-in data is kept per library; plug-in metadata fields are read back from XMP sidecars; number fields
  compare as numbers in smart rules. (no PR)
- The grid stays responsive with 100,000 photos. (no PR)

## Display

- On Linux, previews use your monitor's profile (X11 per-monitor profiles, with colord as a fallback). (⇠ #1051 merged)
- On Windows, previews use your display's WCS profile. (no PR)

## AI

- BiRefNet Subject / Background masks, running on the GPU with a fallback to the CPU. (no PR)
- SAM 3 can be installed from a local folder (`file://` or an absolute path). (no PR)

## Translations

- German, Spanish, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, Russian and Ukrainian
  translate the AI Denoise screens and the remaining English text (96–99 % of messages). (no PR)
- Toasts, Compare captions, the curve menu, Remove spot mode, the Local menu, Photo Merge choices, the Contact Sheet
  dialog and HDR export rows are translated. (⇠ #1291, #1292, #1293, #1294, #1270 merged; ↑ #1298 open)

## Performance

- Previews build in parallel. Colour noise reduction runs at half resolution while you drag; final renders
  keep full resolution. (no PR)
- Duplicate detection (SHA-1) scans in a single batch. (no PR)

## Testing and CI

- The macOS, Windows and Linux checks run on self-hosted runners, one build cache per runner. (no PR)
- Fast PR gate (`cargo xtask ci --fast` plus the SIL harness); the full test suite runs before a release. (no PR)
- nextest with retries, flaky-test reports and a quarantine list. (no PR)
- SIL harness: the real app runs headless through Lightroom workflows and a seeded monkey test. (no PR)
- Low-value tests were removed across the workspace. (no PR)

## Fixes

- A cancelled video export can no longer hang forever. (no PR)
- A studio-capture scan in another session no longer takes the tethered camera's shots. (no PR)
- DSLR-A100 ARWs open as raws, not as TIFFs. (no PR)
- A malformed SRW row table can no longer overflow. (no PR)
- Renames move XMP sidecars by the exact name the folder lists (case-insensitive volumes). (no PR)
- The brush header row wraps when the right panel is narrow. Up / Down over a slider nudge the slider instead
  of changing the photo. (no PR)
- Fixed the Japanese "quit to finish" message and locale catalogs that had broken JSON. (no PR)
- Fixed web build and platform-specific test failures on macOS and Windows. (no PR)
