# 26.47.2

## Develop and masking

- **Copy some of the masks:** Copy Settings lists each mask of the photo under Masking with its name and what it is
  made of, so you copy only the masks you tick; the pasted masks stay linked. A new mask whose automatic name
  another mask already has gets a number ("Brush 2"). `develop.copy` takes `masks` (mask ids). Issue #1268.

## Develop

- **Camera look fitted on the vignetting the camera JPEG shows:** the per-file look fitted to the camera JPEG now measures how much of the lens database's vignetting correction that JPEG applied and fits on that share, instead of assuming all of it. Canon EOS 6D photos whose JPEGs keep their dark corners rendered 5 to 11 L* under the camera JPEG; they now land within about 1 L* (lens correction off). Photos with a calibration file and cameras with no vignetting data are unchanged. The look version and the thumbnail cache version are bumped.
- **Levels:** a new Edit panel section before the Tone Curve, with a tab each for RGB, Red, Green and Blue. Every channel has an input black, midtone and white point and an output black and white, drawn over the histogram of the photo as the Levels receive it, with markers you drag and number boxes you type into (the midtone is a signed value from -1 to +1, 0 neutral, positive brightens). A black point above the white point inverts the range. On the RGB tab, two pickers take the black or white point from a spot on the photo and stay armed until Esc. Levels sit after Exposure and before the Tone Curve, each channel's own levels first and then the RGB ones. They have their own copy and paste, preset and History group, a Modern layout place on the Tone card, an eye and a Reset, 17 languages, and the commands `develop.levels`, `levels.reset`, `develop.levelsPick` and `levels.histogram` for scripts and agents. Levels are stored in the app's own settings only: nothing is written to `crs:`, so Lightroom shows the photo without them. PR #1252.
- **Brightness:** a new Basic slider right after Contrast, -100..100 (Capture One's -50..50 doubled). It moves the midtones and keeps black and white fixed: on grey it shifts the encoded value in logit space (`logit v = logit u + 0.02495 × C1 value`), on colour the brightest channel takes that curve, the HSV hue is kept and the saturation follows the per-channel result by three quarters. It runs on the encoded display values after the output encoding and before Levels and the Tone Curve, the order Capture One applies it in (after Exposure, Contrast and Saturation, before Levels). Closed form on CPU and GPU (equivalence-tested, SDR and HDR); above SDR white an HDR render applies the same curve rescaled onto the range up to its headroom (everything at or below white is as in SDR, black, white and the peak stay fixed), with a slope kink at white. Brightness 0 leaves the render, the settings JSON and the hash exactly as they were. It has its own copy and paste item (also from an unedited photo), preset blend, undo step and History place under Basic. Stored in the app's own settings only: nothing is written to `crs:`, and it is not mapped to Lightroom's legacy Brightness (Process Version 2010), a different operation; Lightroom shows the photo without it.
- **Plug-in filters remember settings:** a filter parameter marked `"remember": true` in the manifest (ABI v1.1 proposal) starts each new copy of the filter (`develop.pluginFilter.add`, `addTo`, the filter's key) at the value last set for it, kept per plug-in in `filter-remembered.json` in the settings folder; values given to `add` win, the other parameters start at their defaults.

## Library

- **Face suggestions reviewed a person at a time:** the People view sorts the faces into three categories — **Unknown** (raw detections nobody has named), **Suggested** (the faces recognition has matched to someone) and **Confirmed** (the people the library knows) — and groups the suggested ones under the person they look like, with **Confirm all** / **Reject all** for a person's whole group and **✓** / **✗** / **−** on one face (issue #1050). Confirming names those faces that person in one undo step (`faces.confirmSuggestions`); rejecting keeps them unknown and never suggests that person for them again (`faces.rejectSuggestions`, undone from the message with `faces.undoSuggestionDecision`); ignoring leaves a face out of People altogether. The decisions are listed by `faces.suggestionDecisions`, `faces.unnamed` can answer them grouped (`bySuggestion: true`), and `people.category` picks the category from a control surface.
- **Find Duplicates, easier to cull:** each copy shows a larger thumbnail and its whole file name, folder path, capture date, size in pixels, file size and format (wrapped, never cut short). **Compare Group in Survey** shows a group large and side by side in Survey; `library.compareDuplicateGroup {group}` does the same from the control channel. Issue #1254.

## Import, export and formats

- **Nikon NEFX:** the merged pixel-shift images NX Studio writes decode as raw files (three 14-bit samples per pixel, no demosaicing; black level, white balance and distortion correction as for a camera NEF), and the `nefx` extension is imported. Verified on 16-shot merges from one body, the Z 8; 4-, 8- and 32-shot merges and other bodies are unchecked. Previews and thumbnails are binned straight from the samples, so a 181 MP merge no longer needs a full-size picture for the loupe. PR #1267.

## Display

- **Window device loss no longer freezes the app:** when the window's GPU device is lost (seen with Vulkan on AMD integrated graphics under Windows), the library and settings are saved, a message explains it, and the next start draws the window with the next renderer, trying the lost one last from then on. Issue #1194.

## Interface

- **Brush scroll direction:** two-finger trackpad scrolling now enlarges the Masking and Remove brushes when scrolling up and shrinks them when scrolling down (matching Lightroom Classic); an **Invert brush resize direction** toggle in **Settings ▸ Interface** reverses the resize gesture. Issue #1257.

## Performance

- **Large libraries open in seconds:** the search for edited photos (whose previews a full thumbnail cache keeps longest) now runs in the background after the library opens, as **Finding edited photos** in the activity stack, and looks each camera up once instead of once per photo. A synthetic 300,000-photo library with a 16.5 GB thumbnail cache opened in 65–70 s and now opens in about 4 s. Issue #979.

## Updater and security

- **Plug-in author signatures:** `plugin-stamp keygen` and `plugin-stamp sign` add an ed25519 signature section to a stamped plug-in. The first signed install pins its key in `plugins.json`; a later file signed by another key, or unsigned, installs only on the user's yes (the install dialog's **Install Anyway**, or `acceptSigner` / `acceptUnsigned` on `plugin.install`), and an invalid signature is refused. At start-up a plug-in that no longer matches its pin isn't loaded and keeps its grants. `plugin.inspectFile` and `plugin.list` report the signer. Issue #1271.
- **Plug-in updates:** a manifest names its update feed in `updateUrl` (host feature `updates`): one JSON file with an entry per plug-in, matched by `id`, giving the version, download URL, SHA-256, `requires`, declared permission groups, changelog link and a plain-text note of at most 280 characters. Only stamped plug-ins signed by their pinned key get checks, each turned on by the user (off by default) and run when the Plug-in Manager opens, at most daily; nothing checks while it's closed. Only a higher semver version is offered. `plugin.update` refuses a file whose SHA-256, stamp, id, version or signer doesn't match, and installs nothing when it asks for permission groups the feed didn't declare until called with `acceptUndeclared: true`. New commands: `plugin.setUpdateCheck`, `plugin.checkUpdates`, `plugin.update`.

## Release builds and CI

- **One macOS disk image per architecture:** releases add `-macos-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon) and `-macos-x64.dmg` (Intel), each holding one build, beside the universal image, which is about twice their size. The updater takes the image for the Mac it runs on (an Intel build under Rosetta takes the Apple silicon one) and falls back to the universal image. The names are `arm64` and `x64` because installed builds take any `-macos-universal`, `-macos-aarch64` or `-macos-x86_64` file and pick the smallest, which would put the Apple silicon image on Intel Macs; they keep updating through the universal image, which a later release drops (issue #1228).
- **Smaller macOS downloads:** the app and the CLI in the macOS disk images are stripped of their symbol tables before signing, as the Linux and FreeBSD packages already were: about 40 MB less per program and architecture, and about 11 MB less to download for the Apple silicon image. Crash logs still record each panic's message and file:line (issue #1227).

## Fixes

- **Immich locked folders:** Locked Folders… (and `immich.authStatus` / `immich.unlockSession`) no longer fail with a missing-permission message that a key can't fix. Immich keeps its locked folders for a signed-in session (PIN or password) and never opens them for an API key, so the error now says so (`error.kind` `sessionRequired`) in every language; a refused PIN or password is still reported as such. Issue #1253.
- **Search indexing failures:** indexing pauses itself after 25 photos in a row fail, instead of decoding the whole library at full CPU on every start, and logs why photos fail. A task row's "failed" count shows the first reason on hover, and a GPU that returns non-finite CLIP embeddings hands the photo to the CPU instead of failing it.

## Fixed

- **Import: the folder arrow only expands:** a card or volume is listed as a drive and also inside the disk it is mounted on, and the two rows shared one arrow, so a click on the arrow of the copy inside the disk loaded the folder's photos instead of opening it. Each drive's tree now has its own arrows. Issue #1234.
