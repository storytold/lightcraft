# Olympus compressed ORF: shared decoder adoption

The compressed ORF decoder introduced by [LightCraft #3775](https://github.com/storytold/lightcraft/pull/3775) was withdrawn in [#3916](https://github.com/storytold/lightcraft/pull/3916). The earlier clean-room and derivation claims remain withdrawn. This change does not restore that app-local decoder.

LightCraft uses the shared `craft-raw` implementation from [craft-libs #2356](https://github.com/storytold/craft-libs/pull/2356). That record documents the reported [LightCraft #1162](https://github.com/storytold/lightcraft/pull/1162) source, methodology and 23 exact sensor references. The sensor comparisons verify decoded output. Maintainers must accept the implementation's provenance before LightCraft adopts a merged revision.

The shared library owns the decoder, format tests, sensor hashes and reference checker. LightCraft retains app-specific import, render, embedded-preview fallback and offline-proxy tests. The existing generic raw corpus sweep can exercise all 23 reference files when they are available. This is measured sample coverage, not a claim of every camera mode, colour calibration or Lightroom rendering parity. Headline and per-model verification counts remain unchanged pending acceptance and the separate coverage process.

## Existing libraries

ORF support is rechecked when a record's checked decoder identity differs from the running decoder. Legacy app-version-only stamps are stale even when the app version is unchanged, so opening an existing library automatically rechecks its ORFs in the background, including Local browsing records. Fresh results keep the decoder identity and are not rechecked on every open. Future ORF compatibility changes must update `lightcraft_raw::ORF_DECODER_VERSION`, which is also used by pixel-cache keys. Other preview-only raw formats retain their app-version checks. **Reload from Disk** (`photo.reload`) and **Recheck Preview-Only Raws** (`library.recheckPreviewOnly` with `all: true`) can still force a check. An incorrectly labeled old record without a preview-only flag needs Reload while its original is online: the recheck cannot identify its container from an offline hash. Missing or offline originals keep their recorded state; reconnect them before rechecking. Existing edits remain, though applying them to sensor data can look different from applying them to the camera JPEG.

ORF render and smart-preview identities change with this decoder. Smart previews also distinguish sensor pixels from preview-derived pixels, so a late worker or Undo cannot overwrite another interpretation's offline image. Content-only legacy sensor-ORF proxies are retained on disk but cannot be loaded: even `raw: true` facts cannot establish which older decoder wrote their pixels. Reconnect the original and use **Build Smart Previews** to rebuild the current identity. The old files also continue to protect Local records from automatic forgetting. Legacy preview-derived ORF proxies with matching facts remain usable, including after Undo; matching-facts proxies for other raw formats and ordinary JPEG proxies without facts keep their compatibility.

Unsupported or damaged sensor data can still use an intact embedded preview. Those pixels retain rendered-image semantics for editing and export; a file without a usable sensor image or preview reports an error. Git history and the withdrawal record remain available.
