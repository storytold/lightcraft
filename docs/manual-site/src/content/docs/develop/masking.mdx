<Steps>

1. **Open the Masks tool.** The panel offers every way to make a mask.

   <Shot name="mask-1-open" alt="The Masks tool open, Create New Mask choices" />

2. **Pick a type and mark the area.** Here: **Linear**, then drag on the photo from the top edge down to where the effect should fade out.

   <Shot name="mask-2-linear" alt="A linear mask dragged on the sky" />

3. **Adjust the mask.** Its own Light, Colour, Color Grading, Effects and Detail sliders only touch the masked area. Colour's **Hue** is an angle in degrees: about ±34° (Lightroom's range), or ±180° with **Settings ▸ Interface ▸ Expanded sliders** on.

   <Shot name="mask-3-adjust" alt="Exposure, temperature, saturation and dehaze changed for the sky mask" />

4. **Add another mask.** Pick **Radial** and drag out from the centre of the sun. Each mask is listed in the panel.

   <Shot name="mask-4-radial" alt="A radial mask around the sun" />

5. **Leave the tool** to see the result without the overlay. After a brush stroke the overlay stays on until a mask slider moves.

   <Shot name="mask-5-result" alt="The finished photo with both masks applied" />

</Steps>

- **Delete**, **X** and the tool keys act only on a tool that is on screen, not on a saved panel in the grid, Compare, Survey or full-screen preview.

- **Create from the photo**: Object, Describe (text prompt), Subject, Sky, Background, People, Remembered (reuse a saved selection). AI selections need their model installed.
- **Draw**: Brush, Linear, Radial.
  - Resize the brush with `[` / `]`, the mouse wheel, or two-finger trackpad scrolling (scrolling up enlarges the brush, matching Lightroom Classic). To turn the scroll gesture the other way, enable **Invert brush resize direction** under **Settings ▸ Interface**.
  - By keyboard: Tab to the brush handle on the photo, **Return** puts the brush down and the arrow keys paint ([Accessibility](../../platforms/accessibility/)).
- **Style Brush** (from Capture One): under the Create New Mask tiles, once you have saved an effect preset. Pick an effect and a new Brush mask starts with its sliders (and its brush settings) already set; paint, and the effect goes where you paint. One undo removes the empty mask again. Command: `mask.styleBrush`.

  <NeedsImage what="The Style Brush menu under Create New Mask, listing effect presets by group" />

- **Range**: Luminance, Color, Depth (needs the Depth Anything model; see [AI masks](../../ai/masks-ai/#depth)).
- **Before any mask exists**: the panel shows the settings the next mask will start from ("Settings for the next mask"), dimmed and inert. Create a mask first; once one exists those sliders work, and with masks present but none selected they again set up the next mask.
- **Colour tools**: under Colour's sliders are **Color** (paint-bucket icon), **Color Mixer** (palette icon) and **Point Color** (eyedropper icon). **Color Grading** is its own section right under Colour, with the colour-wheels icon. The Targeted Adjustment buttons (Tone Curve, Color Mixer, the mask's Color Mixer) show a reticle.
- **Solo Mode**: right-click any mask section title and tick **Solo Mode** (or **Window ▸ Edit Sections ▸ Solo Mode in Masks**). Opening one section then folds the others. Alt-click a section title to open just that one with the mode off.

  <NeedsImage what="A mask's sections in Solo Mode: only Colour open, with the Color, Color Mixer and Point Color rows and their icons" />

- **Color** (the swatch under Saturation): tints the masked area. Open it for **Hue** (0–360°, round the colour wheel) and **Saturation** (how strongly); Saturation 0 is no tint.
- Add, subtract and intersect to refine; **Refine Edges** and **Feather** soften the border.
- Masks use image-relative coordinates, so previews and exports match at any size.

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  **Masks** is in the dock. Show or hide the mask overlay: **View ▸ Tool Overlay**. Brush size and feather: **Window ▸ Tools**.
</Aside>
