Where a raw's sensor clipped one or more colours, the missing values are rebuilt before white balance, so bright
areas do not turn magenta or cyan when you darken them with **Highlights**, **Whites** or **Exposure**. Fully blown
areas always come out neutral.

Choose the method in the Edit panel: **Detail ▸ Sensor ▸ Highlights** (raws only).

- **Rebuild** (the default): the colour of reliable, unclipped detail nearby, carried into the clipped area; white
  where there is none.
- **Inpaint Opposed**: a clipped colour is rebuilt from the two others, corrected by the colour of the unclipped
  surroundings of all clipped areas. Fast; good for small specular highlights and evenly lit skies. Ported from
  darktable.
- **Guided Laplacian**: the clipped area is painted in from its edges over many passes, following the texture of the
  colour that kept the most detail. Slower; keeps texture and colour gradients in larger clipped areas (clouds, lit
  skin). Ported from darktable.

The choice is saved with the photo (`sensor.highlightMode`: 0 Rebuild, 1 Inpaint Opposed, 2 Guided Laplacian), so it
syncs, copies, pastes and goes into presets like any other setting, and the CLI and MCP set it with `develop.set`.
Previews, 1:1 and exports use the same method.
