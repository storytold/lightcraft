### Selecting mask pins with the brush active
- Hovering, clicking, or dragging a mask or component pin while the Brush tool (or an armed Color Range or Object tool) is active now shows the move cursor and selects or moves the pin instead of painting or starting a new mask over it (issue #1161).
- Releasing a brush stroke requests the updated mask overlay on the same frame and keeps the live additive preview until the re-rendered view arrives (issue #1161).
