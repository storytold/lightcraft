### Lens corrections and Transform on video

- A video clip now takes Lens Corrections (distortion, vignetting, chromatic aberration, a lens profile) and Transform (Upright Auto, Level, Vertical, Full and the Transform sliders), like a photo. Every frame gets the same correction: in the loupe while it plays, in Capture Frame and in the exported movie.
- Upright looks at the clip's poster frame once; pick the poster frame first (Set Poster Frame).
