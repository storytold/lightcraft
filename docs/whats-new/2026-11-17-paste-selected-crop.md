### Paste Selected Settings pastes the crop and Remove
- Copy Edit Settings still leaves Crop, Remove, Masking and Red Eye out by default, so a plain Paste doesn't change
  them. Ticking one of them in Paste Selected Settings (⇧⌘V) now pastes it from the copied photo; before, a group the
  copy had left out could not be ticked.
- A crop pasted or pasted from the previous photo onto a photo of another shape keeps its shape, angle, tightness and
  place instead of being stretched, as Sync Settings and Auto Sync already did. An Original lock takes the new photo's
  own shape.
