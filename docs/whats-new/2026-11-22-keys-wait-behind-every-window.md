### Shortcuts wait behind every window that waits for you

- Keys no longer act on the photos behind Find Duplicates, Review Comments, Import Warnings, the Plug-in Manager, the Plug-in Marketplace, the Identity Plate Editor, the catalog dialogs, the Export sheet, or a question such as quitting with unsaved changes: a number key no longer rates the photo, Esc no longer takes the loupe back to the grid behind them, and holding `\` no longer shows the Before view. Clicks wait too: the photos and panels behind these windows don't respond until they close. On macOS the menu bar's commands are greyed out until the window closes, as for a dialog; ⌘Q still quits.
- In the Plug-in Marketplace, the Plug-in Manager and the Edit In windows, Esc in a text field leaves the field first; the next Esc closes the window.
- On Android, Back closes Find Duplicates, Review Comments, Import Warnings and the Identity Plate Editor, as it closes a dialog, instead of acting on what's behind them or leaving the app.
- The Welcome window, and the window of a catalog that couldn't be opened, leave the menu bar working, so Settings, About and Check for Updates stay in reach.
