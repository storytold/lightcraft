### Adaptive presets

- Presets can carry masks as recipes (subject, sky, background, people, objects); applying one selects them again on each photo.
- A new built-in **Smart Masks: Adaptive** group: Subject Pop, Deep Sky, Quiet Background, Subject and Sky. Such presets show an **AI** marker in the Presets panel.
- In a build without the AI model, the rest of the preset still applies and a message names the masks it skipped.
