### Steadier camera colour for converted DNGs

- A camera calibration fitted to Lightroom that can't beat the colour the app already fits to each raw's camera JPEG is now kept for that camera's converted DNGs, which get no such fit, instead of being refused.
- The **Calibrated camera colour** option no longer disappears and reappears while a photo of the same camera loads: it stays in place, greyed out, so the Develop panel doesn't jump. The **Fix Hot and Dead Pixels** count line keeps its place too.
