### Copy only the masks you want
- In Copy Settings, open Masking with its arrow to see each mask of the photo, its name and what it is made of
  ("Mask 2: Radial Gradient, Brush"). Tick the ones to copy: AI masks that carry over to other photos, say,
  while a brush mask stays behind. The pasted masks stay linked to the photo they came from (issue #1268).
- A new mask whose automatic name another mask of the photo already has gets a number ("Brush 2"), so every mask
  in the list has its own name. Names you gave and names from Lightroom stay as they are.
