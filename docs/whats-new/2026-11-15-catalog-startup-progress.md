### Progress while the catalog opens
- The window now says **Opening your catalog** while it opens, instead of sitting silent: after an update, the catalog's format upgrade runs behind that window with the bytes of its safety copy as a moving bar, so a wait after an update says what it is doing.
- The catalog opens after the window appears (it used to open before, leaving a blank window on a large or just-updated catalog); a catalog that can't be opened still ends at the usual window with Try Again.
