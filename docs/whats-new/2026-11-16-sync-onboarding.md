### Simpler sync setup
- Settings ▸ Sync lists each collection on one row with its choice at the right, so nothing runs off a phone screen; the Immich albums fold under one line with how many this device keeps.
- Each device's photos in no collection are told apart: "Not in a collection on ‹device›".
- The Immich card shows Sync Now, Import…, Choose Albums… and Metadata Sync…; the rest is under More. "Sync Immich albums" (was "Show Immich albums as collections") says it keeps chosen albums in step both ways.
- Importing the camera roll adds it in place and offers "Keep adding new photos from the camera roll", and says where those photos go.
- On Android, tap the Library's title to show a collection.
- Turning on "Sync this device's camera roll" on Android now watches the camera roll too, so the collection fills.
