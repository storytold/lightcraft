### Real colour matrices for about 400 newer cameras

- Raws from about 400 cameras released after 2018 (Nikon Z 7, Z 9, Z 5, Z 50, Canon EOS R3, R7, R100, Fujifilm GFX 100 II, X-H2, Panasonic S5, GH6 and many more) now start from LibRaw's colour matrices instead of a neutral one when the camera JPEG fit is not used.
