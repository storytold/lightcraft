### Face suggestions reviewed a person at a time
- The People view has three categories: **Unknown** faces, the **Suggested** faces recognition matched to someone, and the **Confirmed** people it knows.
- Suggested faces are grouped under the person they look like, with **Confirm all** and **Reject all** for their whole group, or **✓**, **✗** and **−** on one face (issue #1050).
- Confirming names those faces that person in one undo step; rejecting keeps them unknown, still found by the next scan, and stops that person being suggested for them again (Undo is on the message).
