### Android: very large photos open, AI stays off
- Opening a 200 MP phone photo no longer runs out of memory: when the full size does not fit, the loupe zoomed in and the crop view use the photo decoded at a half, a quarter or an eighth of its size; exports still use the full size.
- On Android, Semantic search is off until you switch it on, and an installed model is no longer loaded at startup while it is off.
- Building previews in the background takes far less memory on a phone, and happens only while you browse: opening a photo in Develop pauses it and frees memory for the photo you edit.
- Touch layout: tapping a photo in the grid opens that photo, never the one opened last.
