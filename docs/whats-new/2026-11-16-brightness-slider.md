### Brightness
- A new Brightness slider in the Basic section, right after Contrast: it lifts or darkens the midtones while black and white stay where they are, so nothing clips. Positive values lift the shadows and midtones, negative values darken them.
- It works on the colours too: the brightest channel follows the curve, the hue is kept, and the saturation follows it most of the way, so lifted colours do not turn grey and darkened ones do not turn muddy.
- Brightness copies and pastes, goes into presets (and blends with the Amount slider), shows in History, and has the same reset, keyboard and wheel behaviour as the other sliders. A photo without Brightness pasted onto one that has it resets it.
- This Brightness is its own operation, not a mapping of Lightroom's old Brightness slider (Process Version 2010). It stays in the app's own settings and is not written to the XMP `crs:` settings, so Lightroom shows the photo without it.
