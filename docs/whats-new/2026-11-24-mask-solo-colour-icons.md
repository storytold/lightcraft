### Mask sections: Solo Mode, Color Grading under Colour, clearer icons

- A mask's sections have a **Solo Mode**: right-click a section title, or **Window ▸ Edit Sections ▸ Solo Mode in Masks**. Opening one section folds the others; Alt-click opens just one.
- **Color Grading** is now a section of its own right under Colour, with the colour-wheels icon. Colour's tools have distinct icons: a paint bucket for Color, a palette for Color Mixer, an eyedropper for Point Color.
- The Targeted Adjustment buttons (Tone Curve, Color Mixer, masks) show a reticle instead of the picker-and-arrow.
