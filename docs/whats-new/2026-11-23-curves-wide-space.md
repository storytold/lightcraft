### Tone Curve channel curves act as in Lightroom

- New process **V10**: the Tone Curve works in a fixed wide colour space before the export conversion, so strong red, green and blue channel curves imported from Lightroom (a scan's colour-cast fix, for example) now do what they did there, and an edit looks the same exported to sRGB, Display P3 or ProPhoto. Older photos keep their look until you choose **Update to Current Process**.
