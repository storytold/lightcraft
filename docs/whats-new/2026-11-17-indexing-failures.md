### Search indexing stops when every photo fails
- If photos keep failing to index for semantic search, indexing now pauses itself instead of working through the whole library on every start. Resume it from its row or in Settings ▸ AI Models.
- Hover the "failed" count on a task's row to see why the first photo failed.
- If the graphics card gives bad results for a photo, the CPU takes over instead of the photo failing.
