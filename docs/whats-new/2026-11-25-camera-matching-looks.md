### Camera Matching looks from your own RAW + JPEG pairs

- Select raws together with the camera's JPEGs of the same names and choose Photo ▸ Develop Settings ▸ Create Camera Matching Look from Raw + JPEG: the camera's own tone and colour are fitted and kept as a profile in the Profile Browser's new Camera Matching group, with an Amount slider.
- Shoot the JPEGs in another picture style to get a look for that style. Only the fitted table is kept, nothing of the photos.
