### Getting Started guide

- After the first start's Welcome window, a short guide walks through the app one page at a time. **Help ▸ Getting Started…** opens it again.
- **Choose AI features**: each AI download with its size, licence, memory use and speed, next to this computer's memory and graphics card. What is ticked at first depends on the computer; face recognition and AI search are never ticked for you. Nothing downloads until **Finish**, and then in the background.
- **Interface**: layout (Classic, Modern, Compact), light or dark and the colour scheme, text size, language and grading mode, shown at once, with **Undo these changes**.
- **Key settings**: which library opens at start, update checks, XMP sidecars and Reduce motion.
- A page each for Import, Library, Develop, the AI tools, Map/Book/Slideshow/Print/Web and Export and sync, each with a link to its manual page. **Skip** or Esc closes the guide on any page without downloading anything.
