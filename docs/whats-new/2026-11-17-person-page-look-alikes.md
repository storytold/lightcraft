### A person's page no longer offers someone else's faces
- A person's suggested faces now leave out faces that look as much or more like someone else already named, so
  when two people look alike, each page offers that person's own faces. Poor faces (tiny, blurred, from behind)
  that look a little like everybody no longer fill the pages of people with many photos.
- New in Settings ▸ Faces ▸ Matching: **Ignore a person's odd faces (from behind, misnamed)**, off by default. A
  person's faces far from their usual look (a head from behind, someone else named by mistake) no longer count when
  their page looks for faces like theirs. It can also leave out photos where they look very different (as a child,
  with a beard), so it is best used for a while in a large library.
