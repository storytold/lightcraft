### Nikon NEFX files open
- Nikon NEFX files, the merged pixel-shift images NX Studio writes from a series of shots, are now imported and edited as raw files instead of showing only their preview. Checked on 16-shot merges from the Z 8; other shot counts and cameras are not checked yet.
- Photos already in a library as preview-only come back as editable after Reload from Disk, or when the app next rechecks its preview-only raws.
