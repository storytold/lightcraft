### HDR preview on the Export screen
- The Export screen previews the first photo as its file will be: the export's size, output sharpening and watermark, with the file's size under it. It renders in the background and never holds up Export.
- **SDR | HDR** in the preview: SDR shows the SDR rendition; HDR shows the HDR rendition of an HDR export on an HDR display.
- Where HDR can't be shown, the preview keeps the SDR rendition and says why, and what to turn on.
- A wide window shows the preview beside the settings; a narrow one at the end of the settings page.
