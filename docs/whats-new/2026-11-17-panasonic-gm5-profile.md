### Built-in Panasonic DMC-GM5 colour profile
- Panasonic DMC-GM5 raws take their colour from a built-in profile pooled from 1,520 photos and their camera JPEGs,
  instead of a fit to each photo's own JPEG. Previews of GM5 raws are rebuilt once.
- Known limits: a GM5 raw shot with the camera's monochrome photo style still renders in colour, and some orange
  tones lean towards pink.
