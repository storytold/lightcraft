### Device sync: the whole catalog or chosen collections
- Settings ▸ Sync is rebuilt: name this device, pair with a code both screens show, then sync the open catalog with a paired device, either the whole catalog or collections only.
- Per collection, the owning device chooses None, Allow Preview, Allow Serve or Allow Sync; other devices choose None, Previews, Full Quality or Full Sync.
- Photos and their originals or previews now travel, not only edits.
- Grid cells and collection rows show a small pill, such as RF ↓: where the original is, how much of it is here, and which way changes still travel.
- A collection created while the catalog syncs is asked about at once, and the top bar's cloud shows a count while an invitation or a question waits.
