### Speed-edit keys
- In Develop, hold `E` and scroll or drag sideways on the photo to change Exposure; `C`, `H`, `S`, `W` and `B` do
  Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites and Blacks. Each gesture is one undo step, and a quick tap still runs the
  key's shortcut. `app.setSpeedKey` gives any key to any Develop slider.
