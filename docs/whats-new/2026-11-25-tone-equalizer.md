### Tone Equalizer
- A new Tone Equalizer section in the Edit panel, between Levels and the Tone Curve: nine sliders change the exposure of the deep shadows, the shadows, and so on up to middle grey, one stop band at a time.
- Preserve Details keeps the texture inside a band, so lifting the shadows does not flatten them; Mask Size, Edge Refinement and Curve Smoothing tune how the bands follow the photo.
- It copies and pastes, goes into presets, shows in History and has an eye and a reset. Lightroom does not know it: a photo edited with it shows without it there.
