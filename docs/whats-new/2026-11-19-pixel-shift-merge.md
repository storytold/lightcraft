### Pixel Shift merge

- **Photo ▸ Photo Merge ▸ Pixel Shift** merges the frames of a Nikon Z 8 pixel-shift series (16 shots) into one image with full colour at every pixel, on the raw data before demosaicing, and adds it to the library stacked on its frames. It opens like a freshly imported raw: no edits, and the white balance, camera profile and colour look work as they do for the camera's own raw.
- A selection that is not one complete series is refused with the reason, and a series that already has its merged file is not merged again. Choosing HDR, Panorama or Focus Stack on a complete series asks whether to merge it as Pixel Shift instead.
- **Delete Merge Sources** moves the merged photo's frames other than frame 01 to Recently Deleted.
