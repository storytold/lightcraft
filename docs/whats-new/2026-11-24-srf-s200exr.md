### Sony DSC-F828 and FinePix S200EXR raws open

- Sony SRF files from the DSC-F828 (and the DSC-V3, not yet checked on a real file) now open from the sensor data instead of the small embedded preview. The F828's emerald photosites are read as green, so its colour is approximate.
- Fujifilm FinePix S200EXR / S205EXR RAFs now open from the sensor data.
