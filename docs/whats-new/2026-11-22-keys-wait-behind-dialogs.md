### Shortcuts wait while a dialog is open

- On macOS, menu shortcuts such as ⌘=, ⌘- and ⌘Delete no longer act on the photo behind Settings or another dialog. The menu bar's commands are greyed out until it closes; ⌘Q still quits.
- A key that closes the module's key list (⌘/ or Ctrl+/) no longer also runs that key's shortcut.
