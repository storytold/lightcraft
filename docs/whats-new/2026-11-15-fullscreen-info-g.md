### A cleaner full-screen preview
- `I` in the full-screen preview now clears the photo of **all** text when it cycles to Off: the shot-info strip under the photo (ending in the pixel size) goes with the file information, and stays hidden from photo to photo until `I` turns the overlay back on.
- `G` leaves the full-screen preview for the grid you chose (Photo Grid or Square Grid); every view or module switch now leaves the preview rather than changing the view hidden underneath.
