### Levels
- A new Levels section in the Edit panel, just before the Tone Curve: black, midtone and white points per channel (RGB, Red, Green, Blue), output levels, and a histogram with draggable markers and number boxes.
- Two pickers on the RGB tab set the black or white point from the photo; they stay on until you press Esc, so you can click again and again.
- The midtone is a number from -1 to +1 (0 is neutral). A black point above the white point inverts the picture.
- Levels copy and paste, go into presets (and blend with the Amount slider), show in History, and have an eye and a Reset.
- Lightroom does not know Levels: a photo edited with them shows without them there.
