### Loupe and filmstrip backgrounds
- The area round the photo can be Black, Dark Gray, Medium Gray (50 %), Light Gray, White or the theme's own shade:
  right-click the loupe ▸ Background, Window ▸ Appearance ▸ Loupe Background, or Settings ▸ Interface (#1295).
- The filmstrip has its own choice (Filmstrip Background). Both are kept between sessions.
